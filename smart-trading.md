# 🤖 AI Advisor

<figure><img src=".gitbook/assets/ai-advisor.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The **AI Advisor** (app.formion.ai → AI Advisor) is Formion's decision-support hub. It has six surfaces under one tab — Quick Advisor, AI Chat, Trade Vision, Track Record, Research and Strategy Agent — plus a link to your **FORA Neurons** (always-on watchers):

### Quick Advisor — "What can I trade?"

Pick three things — **Style** (e.g. scalp / swing / position), **Risk** (low / medium / high) and **Asset class** — and the AI returns a ranked list of **tradeable ideas** from a live market snapshot, each with a direction and a short rationale. Every run is saved to a **History** list so you can reload a past result without spending a fresh AI call.

### AI Chat

Free-form chat with the AI: ask anything, **attach a chart or file** (PDF, DOCX, XLS, TXT or images), pick the model, and toggle extended **thinking** for harder questions. By default the chat runs through the FORA agent with live market tools; switch FORA off for a plain model chat. **Compare** runs one prompt on 2–4 models side by side (premium models need a paid plan).

### Track Record

A live scoreboard of Quick Advisor ideas measured against what actually happened — per-idea P&L, best/worst excursion, exit reason and win-rate by model — so you can judge the advisor by its results, not its confidence.

### Trade Vision

Turn a **chart screenshot or a sentence** into a structured read (bias, entry zone, stop, take-profits, key levels). Full details on the **[Trade Vision](trade-vision.md)** page.

### Research

Cited research reports and a Portfolio Builder, delivered as a PDF. Full details on the **[Research AI](research-ai-rag-llm.md)** page.

### Strategy Agent

Describe a strategy in plain language; the agent maps it to a built-in template, backtests it on live data, charts the result and gives you the equivalent Pine Script. Send it to a paper agent or export it to TradingView — and check it out-of-sample before risking money.

***

* **AI budget:** each plan includes a shared AI allowance (Neural $2/month · Pro $5/day · Institutional $30/day with priority queue); daily budgets reset at 00:00 UTC. Connect your own key (**BYOK**) or your ChatGPT Plus account on any plan to run calls on your own provider with no platform budget cap.
* **Multi-model cross-check:** use **Compare** in AI Chat to run the same question on several models and see where they agree or disagree.

{% hint style="warning" %}
AI output is analysis and education, not financial advice. Nothing executes unless you set up a bot, confirm a trade yourself, or switch on FORA Autotrade with your own limits.
{% endhint %}
