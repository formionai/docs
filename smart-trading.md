# 🤖 AI Advisor

The **AI Advisor** (app.formion.ai → AI Advisor) is Formion's decision-support hub. It has four surfaces under one tab:

### Quick Advisor — "What can I trade?"

Pick three things — **Style** (e.g. scalp / swing / position), **Risk** (low / medium / high) and **Asset class** — and the AI returns a ranked list of **tradeable ideas** from a live market snapshot, each with a direction and a short rationale. Every run is saved to a **History** list so you can reload a past result without spending a fresh AI call.

### AI Chat

Free-form chat with the AI: ask anything, **attach a chart or file**, pick the model, and toggle extended **thinking** for harder questions.

### Track Record

A log of the advisor's past ideas measured against what actually happened — so you can judge the advisor by its results, not its confidence.

### Trade Vision

Turn a **chart screenshot or a sentence** into a structured trade plan (entry / stop / take-profits / DCA). Full details on the **[Trade Vision](trade-vision.md)** page.

***

* **AI budget:** each plan includes an AI allowance (Neural ~$2/mo · Pro $5/day · Institutional $30/day with priority). Connect your own key (**BYOK**) on any tier for effectively unlimited usage.
* **Multi-model cross-check:** for important calls, **[FORA](fora.md)** can run the question across several AI models and surface where they agree or disagree.

{% hint style="warning" %}
AI output is analysis and education, not financial advice. Nothing executes unless you set up a bot or confirm a trade yourself.
{% endhint %}
