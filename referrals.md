# 🎁 Referral Program

<figure><img src=".gitbook/assets/app-hero.jpg" alt="Formion workspace"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Share your referral link and earn when the people you bring to Formion make their first real payment — paid in crypto.

## How it works

1. Grab your **referral link** from **Profile → License → Referrals** on [formion.ai](https://formion.ai). Each code has its own link, `https://formion.ai/r/YOURCODE`; you can add extra codes with a label (for example a campaign name) and see clicks per code.
2. Share it. Anyone who signs up through it is tied to you, even if they subscribe later.
3. You earn **20% of the referred user's first real payment**. The commission is credited only after that payment clears the **14-day refund window**.
4. They get **5% off their first payment**, applied automatically at checkout (it doesn't stack with a promo code — the larger discount is used).
5. **Tier-up bonuses:** when a referral moves from Free to Pro you get **$20**, and from Pro to Institutional **$50** — paid as **free subscription days** on your own plan, not as cash.
6. Once your unpaid cash balance (from the 20% share) passes **$20**, request a **payout in crypto** — **USDT (TRC-20 or BEP-20), USDC (ERC-20), BTC or ETH**. Payouts are processed within **48 hours, usually within 24**.

{% hint style="info" %}
Cash payouts come only from the 20% first-payment share; tier-up bonuses are always free subscription days. Your dashboard in **Profile → License → Referrals** shows referred users, paying users, total earned, unpaid balance and sign-ups for the last 30 days.
{% endhint %}

## Rules

* Commission is earned once, on the referred user's **first real payment** — not on later renewals.
* No reward is paid when the referrer and the referred user share a **device, IP address or payment method**.
* Up to **50 active referrals** per user.
* Referral clicks and sign-ups are checked for fraud. Self-referrals and fake sign-ups aren't credited.
* For the separate FOM utility model, see **[FOM token](fom-token.md)**.
