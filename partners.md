# 🤝 Partner Program

Turn your audience into recurring income. The Partner Program is for **creators, communities and tools** — YouTubers, newsletters, Telegram/Discord admins and complementary trading sites — who send traders to Formion.

It's built on the same engine as the [Referral Program](referrals.md), but designed for partners who drive real volume: a tracked link, a full dashboard, crypto payouts, and — for established partners — a **custom higher commission rate**.

## What you earn

* **10% recurring** on every payment your referrals make — for as long as they stay subscribed, not just the first month.
* **Custom rates for bigger partners** — established creators can get a negotiated higher commission. [Get in touch](#become-a-partner).
* Your audience gets **5% off** their first month through your link.
* Payouts in **USDT, USDC, BTC or ETH**.

## How it works

1. Create a free account on [formion.ai](https://formion.ai) and grab your unique link from **Profile → Referrals**: `https://formion.ai/register?ref=YOURCODE`
2. Share it — in your video description, newsletter, Telegram or Discord. Anyone who signs up through it is tied to you, even if they subscribe later.
3. Earn **10%** (or your custom rate) of every payment they make, recurring.
4. Once your balance passes **$20**, request a payout to your crypto address.

## Payouts

{% hint style="info" %}
**Payout SLA:** once you request a payout it is processed **within 48 hours — usually within 24 hours**. Payouts are sent manually to your wallet in USDT / USDC / BTC / ETH. Minimum payout is **$20**.
{% endhint %}

You request payouts any time from **Profile → Referrals**. There's no fixed schedule — request whenever you pass the $20 minimum and it's handled within the window above.

## Who it's for

* **YouTubers & TA creators** — turn a tool review into recurring income instead of a one-off post.
* **Newsletters** — a tracked link with full UTM + dashboard, so you see exactly what converts.
* **Telegram / Discord communities** — pin your link and earn on every upgrade.
* **Tools & sites** — cross-promote with complementary screeners, journals and signal tools.
