# 👾 Trade Vision

<figure><img src=".gitbook/assets/trade-vision.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Trade Vision** turns a picture or a sentence into a structured trade read.

Drop in a **chart screenshot** — or just type a free-form idea like *"long BTC around 62k, stop under 60k, targets 65k/68k"*, or both — and a vision-capable AI returns a structured read: long / short / neutral bias with confidence, an entry zone, stop-loss, up to three take-profit targets, risk:reward, the key levels it saw, warnings and a written rationale.

### How it works

1. Open **app.formion.ai → AI Advisor → Trade Vision**. (On Telegram, `/analyze BTC 4h` in `@formiontradingbot` runs an AI analysis with chart vision on a rendered chart.)
2. Upload a chart image, describe the idea in text, or both; optionally add a symbol hint and pick your style and risk.
3. The AI returns the structured read (bias, entry zone, stop, targets, key levels, warnings) plus reasoning.
4. Decide yourself. Trade Vision is read-only — it places no trades. To act, set an alert, build a bot or ask [FORA](fora.md) to prepare an order for you to confirm.

### Good to know

* **Plain language** — describe the setup the way you would to another trader.
* **You stay in control** — Trade Vision only analyses; nothing executes from it.
* Works with any symbol hint, e.g. BTCUSDT, SPY or GOLD.
