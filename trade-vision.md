# 👾 Trade Vision

<figure><img src=".gitbook/assets/trade-vision.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

**Trade Vision** turns a picture or a sentence into a structured trade read.

Drop in a **chart screenshot** — or just type a free-form idea like *"long BTC around 62k, stop under 60k, targets 65k/68k"*, or both — and a vision-capable AI returns a structured read: long / short / neutral bias with confidence, the key levels it saw, an entry zone, stop-loss, take-profit targets, risk:reward, a **DCA ladder** (3–5 entry rungs with an allocation % each), warnings and a written rationale — in your language (11 languages supported).

### How it works

1. Open **app.formion.ai → AI Advisor → Trade Vision**. (On Telegram, send a chart photo with `/analyze` to [@formiontradingbot](https://t.me/formiontradingbot) for the same image analysis.)
2. Upload a chart image, describe the idea in text, or both; optionally add a symbol hint and pick your style and risk.
3. The AI returns the structured read (bias, key levels, entry zone, stop, take-profits, R:R, DCA ladder, warnings) plus reasoning.
4. Decide yourself. Trade Vision is read-only — it places no trades. To act, set an alert, build a bot or ask [FORA](fora.md) to prepare an order for you to confirm.

### Good to know

* **Plain language** — describe the setup the way you would to another trader.
* **You stay in control** — Trade Vision only analyses; nothing executes from it.
* Works with any symbol hint, e.g. BTCUSDT, SPY or GOLD.
