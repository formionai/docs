# 🌐 Research AI (RAG-LLM)

<figure><img src=".gitbook/assets/research.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

The **Research AI** engine produces long-form, cited research on any asset — grounded on web sources, CoinGecko prices and Formion's own signals, screener, sentiment, Hyblock, GMGN and on-chain data — delivered as a PDF with tables and charts.

### Report templates

Pick a template and the engine assembles the relevant data and writes it up:

* **Comprehensive Analysis** — the full picture in one report.
* **Technical Analysis** — structure, levels, indicators, setups.
* **Market Overview** — regime, breadth, what's moving and why.
* **Investment Strategy** — thesis, time-horizon, allocation ideas.
* **Fundamental Analysis** — project / asset fundamentals.
* **Sentiment Analysis** — news + social tone and intensity.
* **Competitive Analysis** — how an asset stacks up against peers.
* **Trading Strategies** — concrete, rules-based playbooks.

### Portfolio Builder

A dedicated template: tell it your **capital** and **risk level** (Low, Medium, High, Very High or Gambling), and it proposes a diversified portfolio with allocations and reasoning.

### Good to know

* Choose the source (**Web** or **Formion Docs**) and the depth: **Summary** (~2–3 min), **Detailed** (~3–5 min) or **Deep Research** (10+ min). Keep the tab open until the report finishes.
* One report runs at a time per account.
* Daily reports: **Neural 1**, **Pro 10**, **Institutional 50** per UTC day (reset 00:00 UTC); the app shows how many you have used today. See **[Pricing & Tiers](pricing.md#daily-limits)**.
* Reports cite the data they're built on, so you can verify rather than just trust.
* Open it from **app.formion.ai → AI Advisor → Research**, or ask **[FORA](fora.md)** for research in chat (web search and deep research in FORA chat are Pro features).
