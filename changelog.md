# 🗓️ Changelog

What is new in Formion and in these docs, newest first. Each line links to the page with the details.

## 13 November 2026

* **New menu order** — the top menu now follows four groups: **Find** (Quantum Screener, Chart Pro, Signals, Pulse, Coins Hub), **Read** (Data Hub, Analytics, News, AI Advisor), **Act** (Trade, Bots, Neurons, and Dossier on Pro and Institutional) and **Review** (Strategies, Journal, Swarm, Predictions, Academy). Desktop shows two rows, the phone menu shows the group names, and **Backtest** is now called **Strategies**. See [Trading Terminal — The App](the-app.md).
* **Quantum Screener** — the home page is now the **Quantum Screener** with four sections: **Trends**, **Context** (breadth, volatility, BTC regime, unusual volume), **Confluence** (382 combinations tested on history, 0 Verified, five paper candidates *Collecting data*) and **Track record** with **Does a higher score do better?**; new Details visuals (Move since start, Score shape), what MFE / MAE mean, and **What the screener is and isn't**. See [Quantum Screener](screener.md).
* **Live Feed** — a green **LIVE** dot (Live / Delayed / Offline) and **Settings → Live Feed exchange**: Binance, Bybit, OKX, Bitget or Hyperliquid. See [Quantum Screener](screener.md) and [Trading Terminal — The App](the-app.md).
* **Order Book and Watchlist moved** — Imbalance and Walls are now in **Data Hub → Order Book** (Book · DOM · Imbalance · Walls); the Watchlist is in your account menu next to Journal. See [Order Flow & Microstructure](order-flow.md) and [Trading Terminal — The App](the-app.md).
* **Zone Hunter** — the Adaptive brain now shows the frozen **weekly decision** (with its date) next to **Market now** (the same rule on the last closed daily bar); the crypto regime is read three ways (**BTC**, **Altcoins**, **Crypto (all)**); why **Sell the top** is often empty; the **Trend Shift** rule; the Long / Short pill and the paper book below Pro. See [Screener](screener.md).
* **Quantum Screener → Details** — a real candle chart with three moments: **Move began** (hindsight), **Trend start** and **Detected** (when our screener first recorded the trend live); the Trend age ring; forex, metals, oil and indices are labelled **FP Markets (cTrader)**. See [Screener](screener.md).
* **Bias Map** — "neutral" vs "no current read", the 5m / 15m fast lane, coverage on Strength / Sector / Sunburst, session markets scored on Monday; accuracy restarted on 2 Nov 2026 and reads *Collecting data*. See [Screener](screener.md).
* **Quantum Pulse is public** (since 12 Nov 2026) — six plan steps, and **Request removal / correction** on every trader profile. See [Formion Pulse](pulse.md) and [Pulse Trader Profile](pulse-trader-profile.md).
* **Mechanical board, Expectancy Map and Pairs** — how to read every column, the paper bots (0.12 % cost per trade, funding not included) and what each plan sees. See [Analytics](analytics.md).
* **Liquidity Map** — strongest levels with labels (Left / Right / On hover), All / Spot / Perp, pulled count per wall, flow by order size, **Level accuracy**, hatched partly-recorded minutes and a gap banner, FORA wall alerts and drawings. See [Liquidity Map](liquidity-map.md).
* **Liquidation Map** — every perpetual contract of Binance, Bybit and OKX in USD plus Hyperliquid real positions, a per-contract picker and **Map accuracy** per contract (first verdict on 23 Nov 2026, *Collecting data* until then). See [Order Flow & Microstructure](order-flow.md).
* **FORA Neurons** — new **Liquidity walls** neuron (approach, pulled, touch, reject, break). See [FORA Neurons](fora-neurons.md).
* **Bots** — **Polymarket Edge · Live** replaces the retired Whale Copy beta. See [Bots & Automation](bots.md).
* **Signalix** — the Signalix tracker is **archived** (retired on 12 Nov 2026); it no longer produces signals. See [Trading Terminal — The App](the-app.md).
