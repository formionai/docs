# 👨‍🔬 Thesis

### The problem

Serious trading is fragmented. Charts live in one tool, screeners in another, bots in a third, your journal in a spreadsheet, and "AI" is usually a chatbot bolted on the side that never touches your actual positions or gets measured against real outcomes. Retail traders rarely get institutional-grade depth, and the tools that do exist are single-asset and desktop-bound.

### Our thesis

**Bring multi-asset data, AI, automation, backtesting and journaling into one non-custodial workspace — and close the loop.** In Formion, AI isn't a side panel: it reads a live market snapshot, proposes ideas, can place trades on your own exchange (with your confirmation), and every signal it produces is **tracked as a real, back-testable trade** with win-rate, expectancy and equity curves. AI amplifies the trader's judgment; it doesn't replace it.

Three convictions drive the product:

* **Unify, don't fragment.** Crypto, forex, stocks, commodities, options and prediction markets — one terminal, one journal, one set of AI tools.
* **Measure everything.** A signal that isn't tracked to a realized outcome is marketing. We turn every signal — ours and third-party — into a measured trade.
* **Never custody funds.** Keys stay on the user's exchange; Formion connects, analyzes and (on request) executes — it never holds or withdraws.

### Why now

Retail participation, algorithmic tooling and capable LLMs have all arrived at once. The opportunity is to wire them together into a workflow a trader can actually run — from a phone or Telegram — rather than another dashboard to babysit.
